Why Local Equipment Matters

Having our water purification unit near Kirton isn't just convenient – it's essential to maintaining the quality of our service. Pure water is at the heart of what we do. Our system processes tap water through multiple stages: pre-filtration, reverse osmosis at 100psi pressure, and final treatment with ion exchange resin.

The result is water with virtually no dissolved solids – the same purity as distilled water. This ultra-pure water doesn't leave streaks or marks when it dries, and your windows stay cleaner for longer because there's no residue to attract dirt.
