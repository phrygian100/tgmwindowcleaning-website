Why Water Purity Matters

The purity of our water is what sets our service apart. Having a water purification unit in Heckington means we can maintain the highest standards. Tap water passes through multiple pre-filters, then through a reverse osmosis membrane under 100psi pressure, before final processing with ion exchange resin.

The result is water with a Total Dissolved Solids level of 0.1 parts per million or less – the same purity grade as distilled water you'd buy in a supermarket. This ultra-pure water leaves no residue when it dries, meaning your windows stay cleaner for longer with no streaks or watermarks.
