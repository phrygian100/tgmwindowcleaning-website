Our Cleaning Method

We use professional reach and wash systems powered by purified, deionised water. The water is processed through reverse osmosis and ion exchange to remove virtually all dissolved solids. This leaves your windows streak-free and spotless as it dries naturally.

Our equipment allows us to safely clean most windows from ground level, though we also use traditional methods and ladders when needed for particular situations.
